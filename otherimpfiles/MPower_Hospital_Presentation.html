02 / 08 · THE PROBLEM

⚠️ Current Reality

Paper Prescriptions.
Manual Billing. Lost Reports.

Every hospital administrator in AP and Telangana faces the same daily challenges — paper-based workflows that cost time, money, and patient trust.

📋
Patient files on paper
Case sheets, prescriptions, lab reports — all on paper. Files get lost, previous history unavailable when patient returns. Doctor wastes 10 minutes searching for old records every visit.
🧾
Manual billing errors
Bills calculated on paper or basic Excel. GST computation wrong, drug charges missed, lab fees forgotten. Revenue leakage of 8-15% common in manual billing hospitals.
🔬
Lab reports misplaced
Lab technician gives printed report, patient takes it home, doctor never sees it next visit. No centralised lab result history. Critical reports lost in transit.
🛏️
No bed visibility
Ward staff maintains manual bed register. Admission desk calls ward to check availability. Patient waits 30-45 minutes while beds are manually checked across floors.
💊
Pharmacy stock unknown
Pharmacist maintains stock in notebook. Expiry dates missed. Out-of-stock discovered only when prescription arrives. Patient sent to buy medicine outside.
🏛️
ABHA & scheme compliance gaps
National Digital Health Mission requires ABHA ID linking. Arogyasri, Cheyutha, PMJAY patients need digital records for claims. Manual systems cause delays and rejections.
